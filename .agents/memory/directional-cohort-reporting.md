---
name: Directional cohort reporting
description: How signal reports should represent directions that have no observed rows.
---

Always include explicit LONG and SHORT cohorts for each strategy in comparative reports. If a direction has no rows, or either resolved outcome class is below the minimum, mark it `INSUFFICIENT` and do not draw a feature conclusion.

**Why:** Omitting a direction makes an overall strategy result look more representative than it is and can hide that the top-line result is carried by only one side.

**How to apply:** Use a fixed direction set in offline strategy summaries and keep the insufficient reason visible alongside total, resolved, TP, and SL counts.
